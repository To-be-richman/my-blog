Gold surged toward $4,300 per ounce, capping a weekly gain of nearly 6%, and the S&P 500 touched record highs above 7,793 points. Meanwhile, Bitcoin remained stagnant near $64,000. Here is a breakdown of updated price movements, shifting macro catalysts, and the outlook for Bitcoin.

---

## 1. What Happened?

* **Gold Rallies Near $4,300**: Gold pushed past $4,250 and approached $4,300 per ounce, achieving a weekly advance of nearly 6%.
* **Bitcoin Lags at $64,000**: Bitcoin traded sideways near $64,000, displaying structural weakness relative to precious metals and equities.

---

## 2. What Is Driving Gold's Rally?

* **Cooling Rate-Hike Expectations**: Iran and Oman reached an agreement to partially reopen the Strait of Hormuz, easing oil prices. Combined with July ADP private payrolls adding just 44,000 jobs (the weakest reading since January against 70,000 expected), market expectations for Fed rate hikes this year dropped from two to just one.
* **Chinese Buying Support**: China's domestic gold ETFs recorded 14 consecutive days of net inflows, while the People's Bank of China added 82 tons to its reserves over a 20-month period ending in June.

---

## 3. How Are Equities Performing?

* **Record Highs**: The S&P 500 set a new all-time high above 7,793 points before pulling back slightly.
* **Quantified Market Breadth**: Market breadth widened significantly, with 66% of S&P 500 constituent stocks trading above their 50-day moving average and 57% outperforming the benchmark index itself.

---

## 4. Why Is Bitcoin Still Heavy?

* **Key Range Focus**: Traders are focused on the $58,000 to $66,000 range, with breakdown risks remaining if upside momentum stays weak.
* **Prerequisites for a Rally**: CryptoQuant notes that BTC requires stronger spot ETF inflows, lower US Treasury yields, reduced Fed rate pressure, and a positive Coinbase premium to trigger a sustained breakout.

---

## 5. One Big Thing to Watch Out For

**Macro Desensitization and Capital Divergence Behind Bitcoin's Stagnation.**
This week highlighted a stark market divergence. While cooling rate-hike expectations provided a tailwind for both precious metals and digital assets in theory, gold captured the dual benefits of central bank reserves and ETF inflows to surge toward $4,300. In contrast, Bitcoin remained completely desensitized to identical macro tailwinds, staying trapped within its $64,000 range. This demonstrates that without direct spot and ETF inflows, macro catalysts alone are insufficient to trigger a self-sustained BTC breakout.
