---
title: "Wintermute Report: Next Altseason Will Be Highly Selective as Institutional Capital Dominates"
category: "Crypto"
date: "2026-07-31"
---

A new report from Wintermute warns that the next altcoin market cycle will be far more selective than previous ones. Institutional capital is increasingly concentrated in a narrow group of tokens, leading to diminished liquidity and attention for the broader tail of smaller altcoins.

*According to Wintermute data, institutional investors accounted for 72% of its OTC spot trading volume in H1 2026—the highest share on record, up from 61% in H2 2025 and 59% in H1 2025. While this highlights growing institutional influence, Wintermute also notes that the current market environment reflects a broader bear market phase where retail participants are largely absent.*

**Translation:** The market is transitioning from broad retail-driven rallies to an institution-led environment where capital concentrates strictly in high-liquidity assets, though retail absence during this market phase may partially inflate institutional dominance metrics.

**Key Implications:**
- **Narrowing Institutional Focus:** From 2024 to 2026, the number of distinct tokens traded by institutional clients on Wintermute grew by only 24%, compared to a 76% expansion among retail users.
- **Breakdown of Traditional Capital Flow:** CryptoQuant metrics indicate the classic cascade of "BTC profits flowing into small-cap alts" has nearly disappeared, with BTC-denominated altcoin trading volumes hovering near their weakest levels since 2021.
- **Extreme Volume Concentration:** Historical data from Kaiko (July 2025) and recent CryptoQuant figures show top-tier assets dominating the market; excluding stablecoins, the top 10 altcoins account for roughly 80.5% of the total non-BTC/non-stablecoin market cap.
- **Divergent Trading Horizons:** Following sharp token price surges, institutional trading activity typically fades after roughly one day, whereas retail engagement tends to persist for about three days.
- **Retail Absence vs. Structural Shift:** Wintermute explicitly acknowledges that part of the institutional share spike is driven by retail liquidity withdrawing toward equity markets during this bear phase, raising the question of whether a true retail resurgence could partially revive broader altseason dynamics.

**Bottom Line:**
The old playbook where "rising tide lifts all boats" is no longer reliable. While a future macroeconomic shift and retail return could temporarily boost broad market liquidity, the ongoing institutionalization of crypto indicates that the next altseason will favor deep liquidity, clear narratives, and institutional participation over indiscriminate long-tail rallies.
