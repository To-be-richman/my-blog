title: "US Senate Delays CLARITY Act Vote to September Over Trump Crypto Ethic Clause"
category: "REGULATION"
date: "2026-08-08"
---

The US Senate entered its August recess without voting on the CLARITY Act. This landmark crypto market structure bill, **which already passed the House in 2025**, must now wait until at least September. A new political deadlock over ethics clauses targeting presidential crypto holdings has significantly compressed the timeline. Here is a factual breakdown of the delay, the specific political sticking point, and the industry impact.

---

## 1. What Happened?

* **No Vote Before Recess**: Senate Majority Leader John Thune confirmed that no vote on the CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633) would occur before the recess began.
* **Political Opposition**: Thune stated that Democrats are currently opposed to the vote, though he affirmed the bill remains a priority once senators return.

---

## 2. Why Is the Delay Important? The Ethics Deadlock

* **Targeting Presidential Crypto Holdings**: The specific sticking point preventing a bipartisan deal is a proposed ethics clause championed by Senators Thom Tillis and Ruben Gallego. The clause would require the President and federal officials to divest crypto assets worth over $1 million that represent more than 10% of a company’s value—a move widely seen as targeting Donald Trump’s crypto business ventures.
* **Regulatory Uncertainty Continues**: The Act aims to establish a federal regulatory framework and clearly define the respective jurisdictions of the SEC and CFTC.

---

## 3. What Happens Next? Precision on September Timeline

* **Procedural Motions Possible**: Thune may still file a cloture motion (to limit debate) before the recess technically ends.
* **September 15th vs 16th**: If Thune files cloture before the recess ends, the first procedural vote could occur as early as Tuesday, September 15th. If filed after recess, the earliest vote would be September 16th.

---

## 4. Industry and Market Reaction

* **Institutional Disappointment**: Ji Hun Kim, CEO of the **Crypto Council for Innovation (CCI)**, characterized the delay as disappointing, arguing that the lack of a clear framework continues to drive US users overseas.
* **Immediate Market Impact**: Following the announcement, the total crypto market capitalization dropped by approximately 0.6% to $2.27 trillion.

---

## 5. One Big Thing to Watch Out For

**The Pathway is Narrowing and Highly Politicized.**
The CLARITY Act is not dead, but the available time in this legislative session is rapidly evaporating, squeezed by competing priorities like government funding and nominations. By tying the bill’s fate to presidential ethics and Trump's personal business, the path to securing the necessary 60 votes has become significantly more complex.
